## Selected Work

### [Theta](https://github.com/frostyfoxie/Theta)

An experimental AI coding environment focused on **agent orchestration, tool execution, code generation, verification, and autonomous software workflows**.

### [Newton](https://github.com/frostyfoxie/Newton)

An AI-powered learning platform for competitive-exam students, combining **adaptive learning, practice, multimodal doubt solving, persistent student state, analytics, and voice interaction**.

### [Human-Like AI](https://github.com/frostyfoxie/human-like-ai)

An experiment in **human-like conversational AI**, exploring persistent memory, personality, voice interaction, contextual behaviour, and character simulation.

### [Cosmos](https://github.com/frostyfoxie/Cosmos)

An interactive exploration of **physics, astronomy, and cosmology**, combining scientific concepts with visualisation and interactive experiences.

### [Pixelverse](https://github.com/frostyfoxie/Pixelverse)

An interactive science experience exploring **the universe, stars, galaxies, Earth, life, and humanity through visual storytelling**.

### [College Predictor](https://github.com/frostyfoxie/college-predictor)

A data-driven educational tool for **JEE/JoSAA college exploration**, incorporating historical cutoffs, rank analysis, filtering, and college information.

### [Syllabus Tracker](https://github.com/frostyfoxie/syllabus-tracker)

A JEE preparation and syllabus management tool designed around **structured progress tracking and study planning**.
